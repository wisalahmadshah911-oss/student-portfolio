Flip Attack

Capture. Protect. Dominate.

How to play

Red and Blue take turns placing a piece on any empty cell of the 6×6 board. Trap a straight line of opponent pieces (any of 8 directions) between your new piece and another of yours to flip them. The game lasts 25 moves in total. The highest score wins; a tie goes to the player with more pieces.

Scoring

  • Place a piece: +1
  • Capture an opponent piece: +2 each
  • 🪙 Bonus: +3 extra
  • 💣 Bomb: +4 extra
  • 🛡️ Shield: +1 extra
  • ⚡ Double Score: doubles your next capture points

Special cells

  • 🪙 Bonus: just extra points.
  • 💣 Bomb: also flips one adjacent opponent piece you choose.
  • 🛡️ Shield: your piece blocks one capture.
  • ⚡ Double Score: stays ready until you capture.

Each special cell works once, and they are always in the same places.
